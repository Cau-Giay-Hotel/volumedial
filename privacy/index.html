Privacy Policy

VolumeDial is developed for CauGiay Hotel. This Privacy Policy explains what information VolumeDial (“the App”) accesses, why, and how it is handled. It reflects the App’s actual code and configuration, and is kept up to date whenever the App’s data behaviour changes.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at cghotel1206@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • VolumeDial has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server of its own. Your settings, presets, and any image/GIF/font you pick are stored only on your device.
  • The App uses Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) for basic product analytics, crash diagnostics, and remote configuration. These are the only services that receive data from the App.
  • The App does not contain any advertising SDK (no AdMob, no AppLovin, no other ad network) and does not contain any in-app purchase / billing system.
  • The Accessibility Service used to detect volume-key presses does not read, log, or transmit the content shown on your screen or in other apps.

3. Third-party services

VolumeDial uses the following third-party services, and no others:

Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config)

The App includes the Firebase SDK, which automatically collects:

  • Analytics: standard app-usage events (such as app opens and screen views) and device/install identifiers, used to understand how the App is used in aggregate.
  • Crashlytics: crash and error reports (stack traces, device model, OS version) when the App crashes or hangs, used to find and fix bugs.
  • Remote Config: fetches configuration values from Firebase’s servers so we can adjust certain app behaviour without shipping a new version.

This data is processed by Google under Firebase’s own terms and privacy policy (firebase.google.com/support/privacy). We do not combine this data with any personally identifying information, because VolumeDial has no account system to tie it to.

No other third party receives data from VolumeDial. In particular:

  • No advertising SDK is included in the App (no AdMob, no AppLovin, no other ad network) — verified directly against the App’s build configuration.
  • No analytics SDK other than Firebase is included.
  • No in-app purchase / billing library is included.

4. The Accessibility Service, in detail

Android requires an Accessibility Service in order for an app to detect and act on physical volume-key presses while running in the background (there is no other public API for this). VolumeDial’s Accessibility Service is used only to:

  1. Detect volume-up / volume-down key presses so it can adjust the media volume and show the custom dial.
  2. Read which app package is currently in the foreground, so the dial can be skipped for apps on your personal exclusion list.

The service’s configuration also requests the Android capability needed to receive key-event callbacks (a required technical combination on Android), but VolumeDial’s code does not read, store, log, analyze, or transmit anything about the text, images, or layout displayed on your screen or inside other apps — that data is never accessed by the App’s logic. You can review or revoke this permission at any time from Android Settings → Accessibility.

5. Where your data is stored

  • Settings, dial appearance, haptics, gestures, excluded-app list, and saved presets are stored in local app storage on your device (Android DataStore). They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Background images/GIFs and custom fonts you choose are copied into the App’s private storage on your device so they remain available after you close the file picker. They stay on your device.
  • Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage from Android Settings, permanently deletes all of the above from your device.

6. Children’s privacy

VolumeDial is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at cghotel1206@gmail.com and we will address it.

  • You can uninstall the App at any time, which removes all locally stored settings and files.
  • See our Data Deletion page for more detail on removing data associated with the App.
